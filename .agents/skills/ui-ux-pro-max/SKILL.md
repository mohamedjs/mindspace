# UI/UX Pro Max Design Principles

## 1. Neumorphic Soft UI & Depth
- **Soft Shadows & Highlights**: Combine dual drop-shadows (light top-left `#FFFFFF20`, dark bottom-right `#00000040`) for an tactile embossed or extruded feel.
- **Inverted Pressed States**: Inset shadows for active, pressed, or toggled buttons to give a physical tactile response.
- **Subtle Surface Gradients**: Linear gradients with tiny angle shifts (135°) to simulate soft lit physical surfaces.

## 2. Excalidraw Canvas & Sketch Aesthetics
- **Hand-Drawn & Organic Borders**: Slightly irregular border-radii (`border-radius: 255px 15px 225px 15px/15px 225px 15px 255px` or randomized 6px–12px curves).
- **Subtle Grid Patterns**: Crosshatch or dotted background grid overlay (`background-image: radial-gradient(rgba(255,255,255,0.12) 1px, transparent 1px)` with 24px grid spacing).
- **Wobbly Pins & Connectors**: Yarn/string connectors with subtle catenary curves, push pins with specular highlights and soft drop shadows.

## 3. Glassmorphism & Modern Micro-interactions
- **Translucent Overlays**: Backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), subtle translucent borders (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Spring Animations**: High-stiffness, low-damping springs for card lifting, note drag/drop, pin insertion.
- **Color Wheels & Visual Feedback**: Vibrantly colored sticky notes with soft pastel backgrounds, glowing indicator chips, and contextual haptic feedback.
